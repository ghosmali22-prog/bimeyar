RICH INSURANS privacy notice

RICH INSURANS is a private tool that an insurance agency uses to keep its customers' documents. Only people the agency has approved can sign in.

What we receive when you sign in

Sign-in uses "Sign in with Google" with the basic openid, email and profile permissions only. The app receives your name and email address so it can check whether you are on the agency's access list. It never gets access to your own Google Drive, Gmail or any other data.

Where files are stored

Files you upload are stored in the agency owner's Google Drive, together with the uploader's email address and the upload date. They are not shared publicly and are not sent to any other service.

What stays on your device

Your sign-in token is kept in this browser tab's session storage and your language choice in local storage. Signing out clears the token.

Removing access or data

To stop using the app or to ask for files to be removed, contact the agency that gave you access. You can also revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/connections.
